# Code of Conduct

This project is a technical collaboration between people who build different tools, sometimes competing ones. We expect
everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests and any other project space, to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good faith;
- critique ideas and specifications, not people or their products;
- give every participating tool's perspective fair consideration;
- keep private information private: do not post other people's unpublished work, private worlds or personal data;
- accept that decisions may not go your way, and record disagreement in the issue rather than escalating elsewhere.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

If something goes wrong, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile, or open a minimal issue asking for
private contact. Reports are handled confidentially. The repository owner may edit or remove contributions and, in
serious or repeated cases, block participants from the repository.

This code may be revised as participants agree a governance process ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).
